## Brand & Style

This design system blends modern, warm travel aesthetics with crisp iOS mobile patterns. Grounded in coastal earthiness and vibrant aquatic energy, the visual language balances trustworthiness with playful wanderlust.

### Design Style & Personality
- **Warm Contemporary Tactility**: Rooted in high-radius curvature (iOS-style `rounded-2xl` and `rounded-3xl` containers), smooth tactile surfaces, and subtle, airy depth rather than harsh skeuomorphism or cold brutalism.
- **Tone & Mood**: Adventurous, welcoming, reliable, and fluid. Evokes sunlit shorelines, clear coastal waters, and grounded earthy landscapes.
- **Target Audience**: Modern nomadic travelers, experience seekers, and mobile-first explorers seeking intuitive trip management and discovery tools.

## Colors

The color palette directly maps to the coastal travel identity, balancing deep sea teal tones with earthy terra cotta hues and sunlit warmth.

### Primary — Otter Teal
- **Teal-700 (`#084d46`)**: High-contrast interactive anchors, primary buttons, active tab states, and commanding icons.
- **Teal-500 (`#029b8d`)**: Core brand accent, interactive links, highlights, and secondary active fills.
- **Teal-300 (`#56e6d9`)**: Soft badges, icon background fills, and atmospheric visual accents.
- **Teal-100 (`#c6fcf7`)**: Tinted container backgrounds, highlight banners, and selected states.

### Secondary — Terra Brown
- **Terra Brown-800 (`#321d0e`)**: Deep warm anchor, secondary headers, and grounded contrasting elements.
- **Terra Brown-500 (`#a4612d`)**: Warm earth secondary accents, travel card tags, and adventure markers.
- **Terra Brown-200 (`#d5b69e`)**: Subtle sand surfaces, dividers, and inactive borders.

### Tertiary — Sundown Gold
- **Sundown Gold-400 (`#ffc036`)**: Warning indicators, loyalty badges, star ratings, and celebratory accents.
- **Sundown Gold-200 (`#ffe2a3`)**: Warm sunny container fills, promotional banners, and soft notification backdrops.

### UI Text & Neutrals
- **Background (`#f4f4f4`)**: Light neutral base canvas providing high contrast against pure white cards.
- **Huge Title (`#2b2b2b`)**: High-weight screen titles and editorial anchors (Stone-800).
- **Body & Content (`#101010`)**: High-legibility small content, form text, and readable copy (Stone-900).
- **Subtitle & Metadata (`#666666`)**: Secondary timestamps, location labels, and captions (Stone-600).
- **Unselected & Inactive (`#d0d0d0`)**: Disabled controls, inactive dots, and placeholder borders (Stone-300).

### Semantic & Tag Palettes
- **Mint / Teal Tag**: `#00a896` / `#c6fcf7`
- **Coral Red Tag**: `#e63946` / `#ffccd5` (Alerts, cancellations, urgent status)
- **Purple Tag**: `#8338ec` / `#ede2fe` (Culture, premium bookings)
- **Warm Yellow Tag**: `#ffbe0b` / `#fff1c2` (Highlights, tips, pending status)
- **Sky Blue Tag**: `#3a86ff` / `#dbeafe` (Transit, flights, verified status)
- **Green Tag**: `#38b000` / `#d8f3dc` (Completed, eco-friendly, confirmed)

### Expressive Gradients
- **Oti's Ocean**: Linear `135deg`, `#084d46` to `#8beee5` (Hero headers & travel passports)
- **Otter under the Sun**: Linear `135deg`, `#ffe2a3` to `#03c6b4` (Featured journey cards)
- **Oti's Skin**: Linear `135deg`, `#321d0e` to `#a4612d` (Warm earthy storytelling)
- **Golden Coast**: Linear `135deg`, `#a4612d` to `#ffc036` (Sunset highlights & rewards)
- **Under the Sea**: Linear `135deg`, `#321d0e` to `#03c6b4` (Special event overlays)

## Typography

Typography relies on `Inter` across all structural levels to support clarity, legibility, and modern interface dynamics.

- **Display & Huge Titles**: Styled with tight tracking (`-0.03em`) and extra bold/bold weights using `#2b2b2b` (Stone-800).
- **Body & Content**: Set in `#101010` (Stone-900) for standard readability, maintaining a comfortable 1.5 line height for scanning itinerary details and travel guides.
- **Subtitles & Captions**: Rendered in `#666666` (Stone-600) to ensure clear typographic hierarchy without creating harsh starkness.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Employs uppercase or tight medium weights with slight letter tracking for badges, button labels, and navigation chips.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around fluid mobile-first ergonomics expanding into clean desktop views.

### Rhythm & Measurements
- **Base Grid Unit**: 4px base increment with an 8px primary step.
- **Mobile Layout**: 4-column fluid layout with `16px` outer margins and `16px` gutters. Provides edge padding optimized for one-thumb smartphone navigation.
- **Tablet Layout**: 8-column layout with `24px` margins and `16px` gutters.
- **Desktop Layout**: 12-column fixed/max-width container (`1200px`) centered with `40px` margins and `24px` gutters.

### Structure & Content Reflow
- Mobile stacks content vertically with card carousels overflowing horizontally with peek previews (`16px` bleed).
- Multi-column trip overviews adapt to side-by-side split sheets on larger screens.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth combines tactile layering over the `#f4f4f4` canvas with diffused, ambient shadows tinted with soft teal undertones.

### Layer Tiers
- **Canvas Base (`#f4f4f4`)**: The lowest plane. Hosts page content and nested layout modules.
- **Surface Cards (`#ffffff`)**: Raised tactile surfaces featuring a gentle tinted shadow: `0 4px 16px rgba(8, 77, 70, 0.06)`.
- **Active / Floating Overlays**: Modals, bottom navigation bars, and search sheets sit higher: `0 8px 30px rgba(8, 77, 70, 0.12)`.
- **Glass Accents**: Sticky navigation headers and floating filter bars utilize a frosted backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) at `85%` opacity over white or primary teal washes.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates generous iOS-inspired curves that reflect friendly coastal travel themes.

- **Primary Radius Hierarchy**:
  - `rounded-3xl` (`24px` - `32px`): Used for primary cards, destination modal sheets, bottom sheets, and hero banners.
  - `rounded-2xl` (`16px`): Standard for input fields, list tiles, category containers, and secondary action blocks.
  - `rounded-full` (`9999px`): Dedicated to buttons, filter chips, pill badges, and interactive icon containers.
- **Visual Impact**: Generous radii soften the technical density of travel schedules, creating a welcoming, tactile aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid Otter Teal-700 (`#084d46`) fill, text `#ffffff`, height `52px` on mobile, full pill radius (`rounded-full`), with subtle teal hover/tap states (`#029b8d`).
- **Secondary Action**: Warm Terra Brown-500 (`#a4612d`) outline or tinted background fill with Terra Brown-800 (`#321d0e`) text.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent base with Otter Teal-500 text, maintaining `rounded-full` shape for tactile touch targets.

### Destination & Travel Cards
- **Structure**: Surface `#ffffff` on `#f4f4f4` background with `rounded-3xl` corner radii.
- **Padding**: Inner padding of `space-md` (`16px`) or `space-lg` (`24px`).
- **Visuals**: Full-width or top-anchored photography with bottom content container hosting tag chips and price highlights in Sundown Gold (`#ffc036`).

### Category & Status Chips
- **Geometry**: Compact pill-shaped badges (`rounded-full`) with `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal padding.
- **Color Pairs**: High saturation icon or bullet paired with low-opacity tinted backgrounds using the UI State / Category token pairs (e.g., Sky Blue `#3a86ff` text on `#dbeafe` ground).

### Form Inputs
- **Base Style**: Soft off-white or white background with `rounded-2xl` contouring, height `48px` to `52px`.
- **Border**: `1.5px` border in Stone-300 (`#d0d0d0`), elevating to Otter Teal-500 (`#029b8d`) upon active focus with a subtle teal halo.
- **Placeholder**: Stone-600 (`#666666`) typography with leading travel icons.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes**: `rounded-lg` (`8px`) box, filled with Teal-700 (`#084d46`) when selected, featuring a crisp white tick mark.
- **Radio Buttons**: Concentric circles with Teal-700 active dot and matching active outer border.

### Bottom Navigation & Tab Bars
- **Style**: Floating pill dock or grounded edge-to-edge frosted glass bar (`rounded-3xl` or top-rounded `24px`).
- **Active State**: Active tab indicator highlighted with Otter Teal-700 (`#084d46`) icon and label, accompanied by a subtle teal pill indicator dot.
